## What to check

Operational forecast and analysis system, not a homogeneous climate reanalysis. Select model version, initialization time and forecast lead.

Choose a specific version and variable. Check precipitation accumulation intervals, geographic coverage and changes in the observing or modeling system. Add application findings with supporting links. This draft is not an expert endorsement.
