# Workshop name reconciliation

The authoritative [typed list](workshop-datasets.txt) contains 53 names. Every
name maps through `names.yaml` to one or more of the 67 catalog records. The
original 28-row NCAR PDF inventory remains covered. Names, not array similarity,
were reconciled. Some labels identify collections or versions rather than
independent datasets.

Separate entries preserve EDDE v1/v2, CONUS404/CONUS404-PGW, gridMET/MACA,
CESM-LENS/GARD-LENS, and NIU/UW-Mass. CMIP6, HighResMIP, CORDEX and SMILEs are
collections rather than one uniform downscaled archive. Workshop groupings are
preserved in the crosswalk even where a scientific category differs. HRRR is an
operational forecast/analysis family, MSWEP combines precipitation sources, and
ClimateNA includes historical and future estimates.

PNNL6 is identified by the contributor's Chen et al. (2018) DOI. Its original
1981-2015 period differs from the later author evaluation's 1981-2020 coverage;
both scopes remain explicit. NIU means Northern Illinois University; WRF-BCC is
a documented candidate product with Gensini, Haberlie and Ashley provenance.
UW's provider catalog separately identifies the WRF ensemble led by Cliff Mass.

LOCA2-CA and the generic BCSD-CMIP5 archive await exact identification. NIU's
product match remains provisional. These are included as unresolved records,
with contributions invited. The ICAR availability annotation is preserved from
the user list but is not asserted to be a freshly verified access restriction.

Provider links and source locators in each record support the stated facts.
Unknown formats, licenses, dates, training periods and member counts remain
explicitly unknown. No meteorological arrays were downloaded or benchmarked.
